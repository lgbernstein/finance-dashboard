Daily Snapshot — May 20, 2026

For Larry and Maria  |  Prepared by Claude  |  7:00 AM
Markets steady today, but bond yields remain near 18-year highs and a new Fed Chair takes over Friday. Stocks are bouncing back from three straight losing days, but elevated bond yields continue to weigh on retirement account bond holdings. Two major events land in the next 48 hours: Nvidia reports earnings after today's close, and Kevin Warsh is sworn in as Fed Chair on Friday.

Overnight and This Morning

US stocks are rising modestly this morning after three consecutive days of losses. The S&P 500 (the index that tracks 500 of the largest US companies) is up about 0.3%, and the Nasdaq (which is heavy on technology stocks) is up about 0.5%. That means the market is expected to open slightly higher than yesterday's close.

Bond yields eased slightly overnight but remain near historic levels. The 10-year Treasury yield (the interest rate the US government pays to borrow money for 10 years) is at 4.66%. The 30-year Treasury yield sits at 5.19%, close to an 18-year high it briefly hit yesterday. When yields are this high, the prices of existing bonds fall. If you hold bonds in your retirement accounts, those positions are under pressure. Oil pulled back about 1.8%, with West Texas crude at $102.20 a barrel, on hopes that Iran diplomacy is making progress.

Top Stories Today

Bond Yields Stay Near Historic Highs The 30-year Treasury yield briefly hit 5.19% yesterday, its highest level in nearly 19 years. Yields rise when investors demand more return for lending money, often because they expect inflation to stay high. High yields push bond prices down, which is a direct headwind for the bond portion of your retirement portfolio. The 10-year yield is at 4.66%, also elevated. This is the story to watch most closely for your portfolio.
Nvidia Reports Earnings After the Close Today Nvidia is the leading maker of chips (specialized computer processors) used to power artificial intelligence systems. Its earnings results after markets close tonight are the single most-watched corporate report of this quarter. Analysts expect roughly $70 to $78 billion in revenue. A strong beat tends to lift technology stocks broadly, while a miss or cautious guidance can pull them lower. If you hold any broad stock funds, Nvidia's results will likely move your portfolio tomorrow morning.
New Fed Chair Takes Over Friday, First Remarks Will Move Markets Kevin Warsh is being sworn in as the new Federal Reserve chair on Friday, May 22. The Federal Reserve (the Fed) is the US central bank. Its chair sets the tone for interest rate policy, which affects everything from mortgage rates to bond yields to stock valuations. Warsh's first public comments on Friday will be closely watched. Markets are currently pricing in roughly a 37 to 50% probability that the Fed's next move is a rate increase (not a cut). Any signals he sends could move long-term bond yields sharply in either direction.

What to Watch Today

  • Nvidia Q1 earnings after 4 PM ET today. Results and guidance will set the tone for technology stocks and broad market indexes tomorrow morning. Expectations are very high, so the bar to impress investors is elevated.
  • The 30-year Treasury yield. If it climbs back above 5.20%, bond prices will fall further. If it drops toward 5.00%, that would be a relief for your bond holdings. You can track it on any financial news site or through your brokerage.
  • Kevin Warsh confirmation and first public remarks on Friday. Even signals about his inflation or rate-hike views in the next two days could move bond markets before he is officially seated.
