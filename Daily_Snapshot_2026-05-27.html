Daily Snapshot — May 27, 2026

For Larry and Maria  |  Prepared by Claude  |  7:00 AM
S&P 500 hits a new all-time record as oil drops 6% on Iran deal news Stocks closed at fresh highs Tuesday and futures point higher again Wednesday, while falling oil prices ease pressure on inflation, which benefits both your stock and bond holdings.

Overnight and This Morning

Tuesday was a strong day for stocks. The S&P 500, which tracks the 500 largest US companies and is the broadest measure of the US stock market, rose 0.61% to close at a record high of 7,519. The Nasdaq, which is heavily weighted toward technology companies, jumped 1.19%. The Dow Jones Industrial Average, a narrower index of 30 large companies, slipped 0.23% as energy and consumer staples stocks fell.

This morning, US stock futures are edging modestly higher, suggesting the market will open near Tuesday's record close. The 10-year Treasury yield, which is the interest rate on 10-year US government bonds and a key benchmark for the bond market, eased slightly to 4.47%. When yields fall, existing bond prices rise, which is a mild positive for your bond holdings. The biggest overnight story is the sharp drop in oil prices, which signals potential relief on the inflation front.

Top Stories Today

Oil drops 6% on Iran deal; Strait of Hormuz to reopen Reports emerged that Iran and the US have reached a framework agreement that would reopen the Strait of Hormuz, the narrow waterway in the Middle East through which about 20% of the world's oil passes. West Texas Intermediate crude, the US oil benchmark, fell to around $88 per barrel. Lower oil prices mean lower gas prices and reduced shipping costs for businesses, both of which ease inflation pressure and reduce the urgency for the Federal Reserve (the US central bank) to keep interest rates high.
Micron Technology surges 19%, crosses $1 trillion in market value Micron makes the memory chips used in computers, phones, and AI data centers. Its stock jumped 19% after analysts at UBS said they see more than 100% additional upside in the shares, driven by surging demand from artificial intelligence infrastructure. This pushed Micron's total market value above $1 trillion for the first time. If you hold broad index funds or tech-oriented funds, you likely have some exposure to Micron.
Zscaler crashes 27%, drags down cybersecurity sector Zscaler, a cloud security company, issued revenue guidance that disappointed Wall Street. Its stock fell 27%, and other cybersecurity stocks including Palo Alto Networks and CrowdStrike dropped more than 3% in response. This is a sector-specific pullback, not a broad market signal. It does not directly affect diversified stock portfolios or bond holdings.

What to Watch Today

  • Salesforce reports earnings after the market closes today. Salesforce is a major business software company, and its results could move tech stocks significantly. Options traders are pricing in an 8.7% swing in either direction, which is large. If Salesforce disappoints, expect tech-sector volatility Wednesday afternoon and Thursday morning.
  • The PCE inflation report is due Thursday. PCE stands for Personal Consumption Expenditures, and it is the Federal Reserve's preferred way to measure inflation. Forecasters expect it to show annual inflation around 3.3% to 3.8%. A lower reading would be welcome news; a higher reading could push bond yields back up and put pressure on stock prices.
  • Watch oil prices for confirmation of the Iran deal. Tuesday's drop was based on reports, not a signed agreement. If the deal falls apart, oil prices could rebound quickly, reversing some of the day's inflation-relief sentiment.
